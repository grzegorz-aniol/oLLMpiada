ROLE AND GOAL

You are playing a game of chess as White against another AI model playing Black.
Your goal is to win. If you cannot win, a draw is better than a loss. Play your
strongest move: the result counts in a competition between AI models.

RULES

Standard chess rules apply: checkmate the opposing king to win.

A referee enforces these rules:
- Turn limit: each side makes at most 6 moves. When both sides have made
  6 moves, a chess engine judges the final position: a side ahead by at
  least 5 pawns, or with a forced mate, wins; anything else is a draw. Convert a
  winning position before the limit, or build that lead by then.
- Illegal answers: an answer with a missing or illegal move is rejected and you must
  answer again. Your third rejected answer in the same turn loses the game.
- Automatic draws: stalemate, insufficient material, threefold repetition and the
  50-move rule end the game in a draw immediately. The position shows the halfmove
  clock for the 50-move rule and says when the current position has occurred before.
- Opening: you cannot resign or offer a draw during your first 5 moves.
- Resigning: answer with the action resign to give up; your opponent wins. Resign
  only in a hopeless position.
- Draw offers: you may offer a draw together with your move. The offer is open for
  your opponent's next answer only, and any move declines it. To accept your
  opponent's pending offer, answer with the action accept_draw.

ANSWER FORMAT

Answer with three fields: action (move, resign or accept_draw); move (your move
exactly as listed under LEGAL MOVES, required when action is move); offer_draw
(true to offer a draw together with your move, otherwise false).
